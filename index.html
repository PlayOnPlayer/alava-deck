The problem

A family has memories. It does not have a timeline.

Photos, recipes, and voices sit in different places, and a feed is the wrong one. Nothing is arranging them into the life they actually were. The years stay a pile.

Not for a feed

A birthday post is not a record of the year.

No order

Albums, threads, and someone’s memory never line up in time.

Unasked

The story that would fill a gap on the timeline never gets told.

What the archivist does

Turn 1,000 photos, stories, and recipes into memories on an organized timeline.

Photos

The album, the camera roll, the box in the closet, placed in the years they belong to.

Stories

Voices and conversations set beside the moments they explain.

Recipes

The ones that lived in a kitchen, kept on the same timeline.

The Alava AI Archivist does the arranging.

The solution

The AI Archivist builds the timeline as you live.

You talk while you look. You leave a voice, a recipe, a name. The Alava AI Archivist turns each one into a memory and sets it on the family timeline. Later you ask, and it answers from that timeline.

The timeline lives locally and keeps growing. When it moves between people, it moves through the cloud encrypted, and it does not stay there. You own it privately on your device.

What fills it

Everything a family remembers, in order.

  • Photo albums, scanned while you talk through them
  • Voice conversations, kept with the moment they belong to
  • Recipes, the ones that never got written on a card
  • The people in the family, then the extended family
  • All of it placed on one living timeline by the AI Archivist
  • A timeline you own privately on your device

It lives on the family’s devices, not in the cloud. If it has to travel, it travels encrypted, and the cloud does not keep it.

How it keeps living

The timeline grows through conversations.

Some years are thin because nobody asked. The AI Archivist sends a small question, daily or weekly, to an elder, and waits. Their answer becomes the next point on the timeline. The same prompt can open a talk you have been meaning to have.

A question that waits
